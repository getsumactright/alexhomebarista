Two directions for your new site.

Same content, same features, two different feelings. Open each one, scroll all the way down, try it on your phone, and pick the one that feels like you.

A · First Light

Your counter, across one day.

  • Window light moves as you scroll, from 7 a.m. to evening, with a little clock in the corner keeping time.
  • Calm and distinctive. Cool stone, soft sunlight, one drink standing in the light.
  • Each section is a moment in the day: recipes in the morning, codes at noon, brands in the afternoon.

Best if you want a site that feels unlike anyone else's.

Open First Light

B · Glass Strata

Every drink, in layers.

  • Your bio, front and center, word for word from your current page.
  • Drinks as colorful layered glasses that pour themselves in when the page opens, each tinted by its syrup.
  • Bright and simple: Apple-white, with all the color coming from your drinks.

Best if you want your recipes to be the star.

Open Glass Strata

Both versions include

  • Search everythingType a flavor or a brand and get the recipe or the code instantly.
  • Tap to copy codesEvery discount code copies in one tap, ready to paste at checkout.
  • Recipe cards with share linksEach recipe has its own link you can drop into a video caption.
  • A page for brandsPartners, what you make, a spec sheet, and one clear way to reach you.
  • Built for phonesA thumb-friendly tab bar, because most visitors arrive from your bio link.
  • One place to editRecipes, links and codes live in one file, so updates don't touch the design.

What we need from you

  1. Your pick: A, B, or anything you'd like to borrow from the other.
  2. Your recipes. The drinks and syrups shown now are samples so you can see the layout.
  3. A contact email for brand inquiries. Until then, brands are sent to your Instagram DMs.
  4. Your social links and partners, to confirm everything listed is right, plus your tracked Casabrews Amazon links.
