# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (user's choice). Must host on any static host (Netlify, Vercel, GitHub Pages).

## Users

1. **Followers / home brewers** arriving from Instagram, TikTok and YouTube bios, almost always on a phone. Job: find the syrup or drink recipe Alex just posted, or grab the discount code / link for a piece of gear Alex uses.
2. **Brand partnership managers** at coffee, kitchen and lifestyle brands, often on desktop. Job: judge whether Alex is a credible creator to hire (content quality, audience, what's offered) and get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Personal creator site for **alexhomebarista** (Alex Gren), replacing a Linktree. It houses three things: a library of Alex's coffee syrups and coffee recipes, a portfolio/media kit for brands, and a hub for every affiliate link and discount code. Success: followers find a recipe or code in seconds; brands leave convinced enough to email.

## Positioning

A home barista, not a café: café-quality drinks and house-made syrups made on gear people can buy for their own kitchen, with Alex's personal codes attached.

## Operating Context

Traffic arrives from a social "link in bio" tap. Followers often land looking for one specific thing they saw in a video (a recipe, a code). Brands land from an email pitch or a profile visit.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Recipes library: structure only for now. **Alex will supply real recipes**; placeholder cards are clearly marked as placeholders.
- Affiliate hub: real links and codes from the current Linktree (see Evidence).
- Portfolio: partners shown as "Brands I work with". Audience stats, rates and case studies are **placeholders until Alex supplies real numbers**. Never invent follower counts, results or prices.
- Contact: email (address not yet supplied).

## Brand Commitments

- Handle: **alexhomebarista**. Name: Alex Gren.
- Client brief: clean, minimal, Apple-website style, but warm and appealing to people.
- Current bio voice: "Coffee enthusiast sharing my favorite gear and tools for home brewing."

## Product Principles

1. The thing they came for is one tap away: recipe, code, or contact.
2. Codes are the product for followers: always visible, one-tap copy.
3. Brands see craft first, numbers second, and only real numbers.
4. Calm and warm, never salesy.
