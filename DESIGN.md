# Design System: alexhomebarista

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "First Light"**

The site is Alex's counter across one day of window light. The ground is honed stone in shade, cool and grey-green, never cream. A fixed layer of blurred window panes lies across it; as the page scrolls, the light slides, rotates, skews and warms from a near-white morning to an apricot evening, while a live clock in the nav runs from 7:02 AM to 7:45 PM and a 2px day line fills under it. Every section opens with an oversized light-weight time stamp computed from where that section sits in the day. The page itself is the ritual: you read it at the pace the sun crosses the counter.

Everything else is quiet and Apple-tight: one grotesque at negative tracking, near-black ink, translucent nav material, tabular numerals for every spec, hairline rules instead of cards. The drinks are the only colour objects, drawn in CSS from each recipe's layer data as clear glasses and bottles standing in the light, each with a contact shadow raked by the sun. No raster asset ships: there are no photographs and no generated images, so there is nothing to carry provenance for.

The system rejects the coffee-site default (cream ground, serif display, brown everywhere) and the plain tile grid of product cards.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Cool stone ground (#e4e7e2) with moving window light as the only atmosphere.
- Amber is the colour of time: clock, section stamps, the day line, and the focus ring.
- One grotesque (Host Grotesk) at Apple-tight tracking; a mono only for discount codes.
- Tabular numerals on every number: clock, stamps, specs, ratios.
- Hairline rows and segmented controls, not boxed cards.
- CSS-drawn glass and bottle renders with true layer bands and sun-raked shadows.
- Spring physics for the recipe sheet; a single ease-out curve everywhere else.

## Colors

A near-monochrome stone-and-ink palette lit by warm window light, with one amber that belongs to the clock.

### Primary
- **Crema Amber** (amber): the colour of time. Used for the nav clock, the section time stamps, the day line fill, the text caret, the search field's focus inset and the global focus ring. Never a fill behind text, never a button colour.

### Neutral
- **Counter Ink** (ink): all headings and body text, the primary button, the storefront card, the active tab-bar pill, the copied state of a code, the bottle caps.
- **Graphite** (ink-2): secondary copy, subheads, nav links at rest, chip text.
- **Slate** (ink-3): metadata, spec labels, fine print, icons inside fields. Passes 4.5:1 on every ground in the system, including the evening light.
- **Stone in Shade** (ground): the page ground and the browser theme colour. Cool and slightly green, never cream.
- **Lifted Stone** (ground-lift): reduced-transparency fallback for nav, tab bar and search panel.
- **Sheet Paper** (sheet): the recipe sheet surface.
- **Hairline / Strong Hairline** (line, line-strong): row dividers and group rules; the strong one marks the top of a list or a group heading.
- **Tint / Tint Hover** (fill, fill-hover): chip and segmented-control wells, hover wash on links, list highlight in search results.

### Light (not fills)
- **Morning Light** (sun-am): the window panes at the top of the page.
- **Evening Light** (sun-pm): the panes as they warm; cross-faded in from 40% to 95% of the scroll.
- **Selection Sunlight, Amber Glint, cast and contact browns, Warm Caustic, Dusk Glow, Label Shadow, Glass Wall Tint**: light and shadow tints recorded in the sidecar (`colorMeta`), used only inside the window light, the dusk gradient and the CSS-drawn glass and bottle renders.
- **Sun Glint** (sun-glint): the small hot points of light: the sun dot riding the day line, the storefront's outbound icon, the check icon in a copied code, the search-match underline (at 55% alpha). The text-selection colour is a paler sunlight (#ffd49a).

### Named Rules
**The Amber Is Time Rule.** Amber marks time and focus, nothing else. Active navigation is shown in ink (an ink pill on the tab bar, ink text in the nav), not amber. If a new element wants amber, it must be telling the reader what time it is or where focus is.

**The Light Is Not Paint Rule.** Morning, evening and glint colours appear only as light: blurred panes, a dot, a glow, a highlight. They never fill a button, a card or a section background.

**The Never Cream Rule.** Grounds stay cool stone. Warmth arrives only through the moving light and the drinks themselves.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Host Grotesk (with -apple-system, Helvetica Neue)
**Body Font:** Host Grotesk
**Label/Mono Font:** Red Hat Mono 500, for discount codes and the `/` key hint only

**Character:** One variable grotesque (300 to 800) does everything, set tight like Apple product pages: big weights at -0.04em for headlines, a 300 weight for the time stamps so they read as light rather than shouting. The mono appears only where a reader has to type or paste exact characters.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (680, 3.1 to 6rem fluid, 0.98): the hero headline only.
- **Stamp** (300, 4 to 6rem fluid, 0.86, amber, tabular): one per section and the footer; the AM/PM suffix is set at 0.32em, weight 500.
- **Headline** (650, 2.5 to 4.5rem fluid, 1): section instructions ("Make this.", "Copy the code.", "Work with me.").
- **Pitch** (650, 1.75 to 2.75rem fluid, 1.08, max 18ch): the brand pitch line.
- **Sheet title** (650, 1.75 to 2.5rem fluid, 1.04): recipe name in the sheet.
- **Wordmark** (640, 1.5 to 2.5rem fluid, 1.25): partner names set as type, never logos.
- **Footer line** (650, 1.5 to 2.25rem fluid, 1.1): the closing line.
- **Title** (650, 1.5 to 2rem fluid, 1.08): hero recipe name.
- **Storefront title** (650, 1.375 to 2rem fluid, 1.1): the dark storefront card.
- **Hero sub** (400, 1.125 to 1.375rem fluid, 1.4, ink-2, max 34ch).
- **Lead** (400, 1.0625 to 1.25rem fluid, 1.45, ink-2, max 46ch): section intros.
- **Title small** (640, 1.25rem): offer headings; the sheet's spec values use the same step at 600.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.55): running text, list rows, recipe names on the shelf, search input.
- **Body small** (400, 1rem): hero blurb and storefront subline.
- **UI** (560 to 650, 0.9375rem): sub-heads, link buttons, shop links, wordmark in the nav, search empty state, spec columns, toast.
- **Label medium** (520 to 540, 0.875rem): chips, segmented control, meta lines, offer text.
- **Label** (400 to 600, 0.8125rem, tabular): nav, clock, spec labels, fine print, result meta.
- **Tag** (600, 0.6875rem, 0.04em, uppercase): the Sample status tag only.
- **Code** (Red Hat Mono 500, 0.8125rem, 0.02em): discount codes.
- **Key** (Red Hat Mono 500, 0.75rem): the `/` keyboard hint.

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Rule.** Every number a reader compares is set with tabular numerals: clock, stamps, spec columns, spec rows, step counters.

**The Tight Tracking Rule.** Tracking tightens as size grows: -0.005em body, -0.01 to -0.02em at title-adjacent sizes, -0.03 to -0.04em for titles and above. Only the code mono and the small uppercase sample tag open up.

## Layout

A single centred column, `min(100% - 2 * gutter, 1160px)`, with a fluid gutter (clamp(20px, 5vw, 64px)) and generous section spacing (clamp(112px, 15vw, 200px)). The hero is centred; everything after it is left-aligned on a 4fr / 7fr split: the time stamp sits in the narrow left column, the instruction and intro in the wider right column. The pitch and footer reuse the same 4/7 split so the right column reads as one continuous spine.

The hero stage is a three-column grid (label, glass, spec column) aligned to the counter line. Recipes sit on an auto-fill shelf (min 200px) with tall vertical gaps so each glass stands alone; gear is a ruled list of rows (brand and product, code, shop link).

At 900px and below, the 4/7 splits collapse to one column and the hero stage stacks with the glass first and the spec column turned horizontal. At 767px and below, the nav links are replaced by a floating bottom tab bar for the thumb, the shelf becomes two columns, and the code button drops onto its own line under each gear row. The nav is fixed at 52px; anchors scroll with 24px clearance under it.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from light, not stacked cards. The page is flat stone; the only things that lift are glass objects, material surfaces (nav, tab bar, search panel, toast) and the one dark storefront card. Translucent material uses a 0.74 alpha stone tint with `blur(20px) saturate(170%)`; shadows are soft, low-alpha ink, never hard offsets.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Field rest** (`0 1px 2px rgba(29,29,31,.06), 0 8px 24px rgba(29,29,31,.07), inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(29,29,31,.07)`): the search field.
- **Floating panel** (`0 18px 48px rgba(29,29,31,.16), 0 0 0 .5px rgba(29,29,31,.1)`): search results panel.
- **Tab bar** (`0 8px 28px rgba(29,29,31,.14), inset 0 .5px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.9), 0 0 0 .5px rgba(29,29,31,.08)`): the floating mobile tab bar.
- **Dark card** (`0 18px 40px rgba(29,29,31,.16)`, hover `0 24px 48px rgba(29,29,31,.2)`): the storefront card.
- **Sheet** (`0 -12px 48px rgba(29,29,31,.18)`): the recipe sheet, casting upward.
- **Thumb** (`0 1px 2px rgba(29,29,31,.1), 0 3px 8px rgba(29,29,31,.06)`): the segmented-control thumb.
- **Counter contact** (radial `rgba(52,40,28,.26)`, skewX(-38deg), blur 2px): under every glass and bottle.

### Named Rules
**The Sun Casts It Rule.** Object shadows are cast by the window, not by the UI: they skew back and to the right, parallel to the panes. The hero glass's long shadow is driven by `--cast` (-22deg at morning to -12deg at evening) with an amber caustic inside it.

**The Hairline Over Box Rule.** Group content with hairline rules and whitespace. A filled container is reserved for the storefront card, the recipe sheet and material surfaces.

## Shapes

Soft, continuous corners scaled to the object: 6px for key hints and the sample tag, 10px for code buttons and shop links, 11px for search result rows, 12px for the segmented control with 9px on its buttons and thumb, 14px for the toast, 16px for the search field and its panel, 20px for the shelf item's hit area, 24px for the storefront card, 28px for the sheet's top corners (24px on phones), and full pills for buttons, chips, nav links and the tab bar. Render details use the small steps: 2px for bottle-label bars and the bottle neck's lower corners, 3px for the cap and the label, 4px for the neck's top corners. Glasses use an elliptical radius: 7px at the rim and 18px at the base by default (22 to 32px for hot and large variants, set per object); bottles use percentage radii so they scale with size. Icons are a single 1.6px stroke set with round caps, drawn for this site and referenced as inline SVG symbols.

## Components

### Buttons
Calm, pill-shaped, and pressed rather than lit.
- **Shape:** full pill (999px), 50px tall, 0 26px padding, 1.0625rem / 560.
- **Primary:** ink fill, white text; hover deepens to #333336.
- **Quiet:** no fill, inset strong hairline; hover adds the tint wash.
- **Press:** every pressable scales down (0.96 to 0.97) on `:active` with the ease-out curve.
- **Link button:** text in ink at 560 with a trailing arrow that nudges 3px right on hover.

### Chips
- **Style:** tint well, ink-2 text, 34px pill, 0.875rem / 520.
- **State:** hover deepens the well and moves text to ink. Chips are quick actions under the search field, not filters.

### Segmented Control
- **Style:** tint well with 3px inset, 12px radius; a white thumb with a soft two-step shadow slides between options on the ease-out curve (0.42s). Selected text is ink, others ink-2. Full width on phones.

### Discount Code Button
The follower's product: one tap copies.
- **Style:** 38px, 10px radius, translucent white (0.7) with an inset strong hairline, Red Hat Mono code and a 16px copy icon.
- **States:** hover goes solid white with a darker hairline; copied inverts to ink with a sun-glint check icon; a toast confirms. Rows with no code show plain slate text instead.

### Search Field
- **Style:** 56px, 16px radius, translucent white with the field-rest shadow, leading search icon, trailing `/` key hint in mono.
- **Focus:** goes solid white, shadow deepens, and an inset 1.5px amber ring replaces the hairline. Results open in a frosted panel below; matches are underlined with sun glint.

### Navigation
- **Desktop:** fixed 52px translucent bar, three-column grid: wordmark (650, -0.02em) left, pill links centre (ink-2, tint wash on hover, ink when current), live amber clock right. The day line runs along its bottom edge.
- **Mobile:** links move to a floating frosted pill tab bar at the bottom with icons; the current section is an ink pill with white text.

### Day Line
A 2px strong-hairline track under the nav whose amber fill scales with scroll progress, carrying a 9px sun-glint dot ringed in ground colour. It is the one unbroken record of "now".

### Glass and Bottle Renders
CSS objects built from each recipe's layer list (top to bottom, proportional shares): a clear body with a white rim, wall tint and a vertical shine; liquid bands with a faint highlight between layers; optional ice cubes; a frosted base. Iced, hot and large variants change proportions only. The hero glass adds a lit rim on the window side and the sun-raked cast shadow.

### Recipe Sheet
A bottom sheet (max 720px wide, 92dvh tall, 28px top corners) opened as a modal dialog over a 0.34 warm-black scrim that fades with drag distance. It opens and closes on critically damped springs (response 0.42s in, 0.34s out), can be dragged from the grip or the header with 6px hysteresis and rubber-banding above rest, and on release projects momentum (deceleration 0.998) to decide: dismiss past 45% of its height or above 900px/s, otherwise spring back (response 0.3s, damping 0.82) carrying the release velocity. Inside: glass, title, a ruled tabular spec row, then ingredients and numbered method in a 5/7 split.

### Section Time Stamp
One per section and the footer: the time of day that section reaches the top of a typical screen, computed from its position in the page and mapped onto 7:02 AM to 7:45 PM. Amber, weight 300, display size, tabular, with a small AM/PM suffix.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** let the light carry the warmth: blurred panes that move, rotate, skew and cross-fade from Morning Light (#fdfdfa) to Evening Light (#ffe0bd) with scroll progress.
- **Do** give every new section a time stamp computed from its position, and a one-line instruction as its headline.
- **Do** set every comparable number in tabular numerals.
- **Do** use the single ease-out curve `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for CSS transitions, and springs defined by damping ratio and response for anything dragged.
- **Do** honour reduced motion: freeze the light at an early-morning position, replace springs with 150 to 180ms fades, and stop smooth scrolling.
- **Do** provide solid fallbacks for every translucent surface under reduced transparency, and stronger ink and hairlines under increased contrast.
- **Do** draw drinks in CSS from layer data; if real photography arrives later, it should sit in the same window light rather than replace it.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use a cream, beige or brown ground, or a serif display face.
- **Don't** use amber for fills, buttons, active navigation or decoration.
- **Don't** use sun colours as flat fills; they are light.
- **Don't** put content in boxed cards on a grid; use hairline rows, the shelf, or the sheet.
- **Don't** introduce a second sans or use the mono for anything but codes and key hints.
- **Don't** use hard offset shadows or shadows that ignore the window's direction on objects.
